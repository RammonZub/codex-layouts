# Code of Conduct

We are committed to a welcoming, respectful, and harassment-free community for
everyone, regardless of background, identity, experience, or ability.

## Expected behavior

- Be considerate and constructive.
- Critique ideas and code, not people.
- Respect privacy and avoid sharing another person's local task data.
- Accept good-faith feedback and correct mistakes.

## Unacceptable behavior

Harassment, discrimination, threats, sexualized attention, doxxing, sustained
disruption, or publishing private information without permission are not
acceptable.

Maintainers may edit or remove contributions and restrict participation when
needed to protect the community. Serious concerns can be reported privately
through the repository's security or maintainer contact channels.

This policy applies in project spaces and when someone is publicly representing
the project.
